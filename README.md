# Queue2Go

**Control League of Legends from your phone – start a queue and accept matches from anywhere in your home.**

Queue2Go runs as a small program on your PC and turns your phone into a remote control for the League client. No more running back from the kitchen or the couch when the queue pops.

## Features

- **Choose a game mode** – PvP (Ranked, Draft, Swiftplay), ARAM, Arena, Co-op vs. AI, Training and Custom Games. The lobby opens right on your PC.
- **Manage your lobby** – set lanes, invite friends, lobby chat, start the search or leave the lobby.
- **Accept or decline matches** – with a countdown, optional sound and vibration.
- **Champion select** – ban, pick, runes and summoner spells right from your phone.
- **Session overview** – on your PC, counted from program start.

## Download

Get the latest version under **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** as `Queue2Go-vX.Y.Z.zip`.

## How to use

1. Download the ZIP, unzip it and run `Queue2Go.exe` on your PC.
2. **Important:** When Windows asks, allow Queue2Go access to your **private network**. Otherwise your phone can't connect.
3. On your phone (same Wi-Fi), open the address shown by Queue2Go.
4. Start League – when "Connected to League" appears at the top, you're ready to go.

Works on your home Wi-Fi only. Free to use.

### Phone can't connect?

Make sure Queue2Go is allowed through the Windows Firewall for private networks (*Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → Allow an app through firewall*) and that your PC's network is set to **Private**, not Public.

## Is it safe?

- Queue2Go only talks to the League client's local interface on your own PC – the same one the client itself uses for queue and champion select.
- It does not touch the game itself: no reading of game memory, no injection, no automation during matches.
- Everything stays in your home network. No data is sent to the internet, no account or login data is needed or stored.
- Only devices on your own Wi-Fi can reach it.
